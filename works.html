“Works” are series containers. Each series pairs a set of Chinese cultural IP with one or a group of reimagining artists. Shown below as sample compositions; final pieces are created by our approved artists, numbered and woven into silk.

Editions · Poster-as-Art

Treated as a museum object, not a print.

Every silk poster carries an invisible anti-counterfeit number, woven into the silk, traceable piece by piece. The museum label is part of the design — work name, edition, technique, year, but no price.

Two Tracks

Numbered is a collectible; unnumbered is a commodity

Limited Edition

Numbered, never reprinted

Reimagined by the artist and approved by the licensor, released as Edition of N, numbered piece by piece, out of print once sold out. This is the collectible main line.

Open Edition

Unnumbered, reprintable

Unlimited, reprintable entry piece, letting more people own a silk poster. Also a funnel into the collectible line — buy open, collect limited next time.

Open editions never use the words Edition / Limited / numbered. Everyone in the art world understands: numbered = collectible, unnumbered = commodity.
