For Artists

For Artists

Many artists approach us, hoping to exhibit in Macau and publish portfolios. SILKLY ART partners with artists: we provide curation, weaving, release, and exhibition, helping raise the value of your work. Submissions are open; exhibitions are selective.

Why Work With Us

We help you
turn work into an asset

The lone artist’s work often stops at “a nice image”. We add three things: the material foundation of top-grade silk weaving, individual numbering and a tamper-evident provenance, and the market and prestige that come from physical exhibitions.

Material

Top-grade Silk Weaving

Works presented in collectible silk — hand-feel and colour far surpass paper print.

Trust

Numbering + Registry

Every piece has an identity and a traceable record, becoming a collectible asset.

Scene

Macau Physical Show

Appears in our gallery space and co-presented thematic exhibitions, meeting collectors.

Revenue Share

Partnership

Artists share in the revenue; appreciation of the work feeds back into your reputation and market.

Submission

Submit a Proposal

Fill in the form below; when you press Send Proposal it opens your default mail client (Outlook, Apple Mail, Gmail web, etc.) with a pre-written message addressed to seanown@gmail.com. Review and press Send in your mail client to deliver it. We assess cultural narrative and craft, and suitable proposals enter the curatorial process.
