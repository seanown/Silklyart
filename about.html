About the Center

About the Center

Macau Longyou Silk Culture & Art Center (SILKLY ART) is a Macau-based international silk art poster house and curatorial institution. Rooted in Macau’s unique position as the crossroads of Chinese culture and the international community, we build a world-class IP collaboration and rating platform.

Mission

Through Macau,
bring Chinese culture to the world

Macau is a port where China and the West have met for centuries — a cultural bridge in itself. We use Macau as the hub to license Chinese cultural assets such as Nantianmen, Palace Culture, and Dunhuang to artists worldwide, reimagined and woven into silk posters the world can collect.

“We are curators ourselves.”

The role of the Center is curation, not vending. Every work is reviewed, approved, released, and exhibited — that is our promise to collectors.

Director

Sean Own

The Center is led by Director Sean Own, who has long moved between Macau, Hong Kong, and Taiwan, working in the silk industry, as an industry advisor, and in association resource integration. He believes Macau’s agility lets him pursue international cultural IP collaborations more freely than a listed company. The Center’s resources come from his accumulated network of casino concessionaires, government, and cultural institutions.

Our Commitments

Four things we will not compromise

Narrow the posters

We make only silk art posters, no other categories. The narrower the category, the clearer the brand.

Licensing relationship

Chinese deities and collection IP are always obtained by “license”, never claimed as owned.

Independent review

The power to rate is handed to an independent system of domestic and international experts and artists, not kept in our own hands.

Every piece exhibited

Every released work has a physical exhibition record, written into its provenance.
