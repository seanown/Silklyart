Basis of Trust · Provenance

Numbered, registered, certified.

The power to rate does not sit with the issuer. We are the third-party recorder: every silk edition enters an independent review of experts and artists, and every piece is bound to a blockchain certificate of provenance for overseas collectors.

Artist submission → Curatorial review → Numbered silk release → Independent certification → Collector market. Secondary-market appreciation feeds back into the artist's reputation.
