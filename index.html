Who We Are

Curators, not vendors

The core role of SILKLY ART is curation. We invite artists to submit, review, and approve; then release the work as a silk edition and exhibit it physically. Every piece carries a cultural story; every exhibition carries a theme — that is our fundamental difference from pure e-commerce or a print factory.

Role

Standing Curatorial House

We curate at our Macau gallery space; mid-to-large exhibitions are co-presented with the government, universities, and cultural institutions.

Path

From Macau to the World

Rooted in Macau’s unique position as the crossroads of Chinese culture and the international community, we build a world-class IP collaboration and rating platform.

Partners

In Partnership with Artists

Many artists approach us, hoping to exhibit in Macau and publish portfolios — we help raise their value.

Material & Technique

Top-grade silk weaving + invisible anti-counterfeit numbering

We work with top-grade silk weaving partners (including museum-grade craft) to secure the material foundation of every piece. Each poster embeds an invisible anti-counterfeit number, visible only under special light — globally unique. This is a selling point no one else can replicate, and the technical basis of our provenance.

Material is the basis of value

Top-grade silk weaving determines the hand-feel, colour rendition, and collecting lifespan of the work.

The number is identity

The invisible anti-counterfeit number sits within the silk, traceable piece by piece, forming the first record of provenance.

Basis of Trust

An independent review system

The power to rate does not sit with the issuer. SILKLY ART establishes an independent review system composed of domestic and international experts and artists, certifying every released work. We are a third-party “recorder”, not someone who scores our own products — that is the source of collectors’ trust.

Artist submissionApproaches us, hoping to exhibit in Macau
Curatorial reviewAccept · approve — not open to all

Independent certificationBy a committee of experts and artists
Collector marketExhibition + secondary circulation
Third-party provenance record: numbering + tamper-evident registry + independent review = the basis of trust. Secondary-market appreciation feeds back into the artist’s reputation.
